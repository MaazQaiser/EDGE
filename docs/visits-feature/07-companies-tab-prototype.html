What this proves

  • The year is one scroll. Pick Downtown Holdings or Meridian Health Group: a quarterly property shows four dates, a monthly one twelve. Nobody chose those numbers.
  • Planned and projected never look alike. Solid dot, route and status above the dashed line; hollow dot and the word expected below it, with nothing to click.
  • Quiet companies are still reachable. They dim out of the roll-up but stay in the rail, which is how you get to a customer nobody is visiting this week.

What is faked

  • Demo data generated in the page from a cadence, the way schedule.mock.js does. Today is Mon 17 Aug 2026 and routes are built through Sun 6 Sep.
  • The other two tabs, the nav rail and the alert pills are painted, not wired — they are there so the new tab can be judged in its actual surroundings.
  • Export and Unassigned → open nothing. In the product they hand off to the existing CSV and add-to-a-route flows.
